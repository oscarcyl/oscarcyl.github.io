### Fellowships & Academic Awards

* **Postgraduate Studentship** — *The Chinese University of Hong Kong* (2025 – Present)
  * Merit based research funding awarded to high achieving M.Phil. students.
* **Gold Award in Undergraduate Research Opportunity Program** — *The Chinese University of Hong Kong* (2024)
  * Highest distinction awarded to the top performing student for outstanding research contributions, algorithmic implementation, and project presentation in the Department of Mathematics.
* **Mathematics Scholarship** — *Department of Mathematics, CUHK* (2021)
  * Merit scholarship granted to top incoming undergraduate students in recognition of high academic standing and potential.
